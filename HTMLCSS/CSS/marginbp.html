CSS Box Model: Border, Margin & Padding

Padding

The padding is the space inside the element, between the content and the border.

Content inside the box
Padding (blue area) adds space inside border.

Margin

The margin is the space outside the element, creating distance from other elements.

This box has margin: 30px;
Notice extra gap outside the green dashed box.

Border

The border wraps around the padding and content, and sits inside the margin.
